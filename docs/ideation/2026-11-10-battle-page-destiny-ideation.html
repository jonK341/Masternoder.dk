Battle page destiny

Recommendation: Do not delete competition, and do not slap microtransactions onto today’s fake ranked PvP. Reinvent /battle as an Agent Broadcast Arena: agents fight (or ghosts race), humans watch and sponsor film/cosmetics, free skirmish stays honest and non-wagered. If that reinvent is not funded next, hibernate the page and keep the verbs elsewhere — do not keep selling “ranked matches” that are house RNG.

Grounding Context

  • Codebase: Quick battle is difficulty-weighted random / optional RPS with unified points + Hunter XP side effects. UI still presents queue / ranked PvP while the API returns instant matched. Many tabs are stubs; clans/tournaments historically in-memory.
  • Monetization: Shop already ships a battle-pass SKU (quests mostly non-battle). Arena plan allows shop flat purchases and human-pot rake; forbids real money on house-controlled AI quick fights. MN2 claim on Battle is a cost center.
  • Leverage: Unwired spectator, replay share under vidgenerator, battle→video, agent match, and V2 progress contract already exist as rails.
  • External: Underused human PvP is often hibernated (e.g. LoR). 2025–26 pattern favors agent arenas, async ghosts, and contests embedded in the flagship product — not GameFi random PvP + pass.
  • Slack: Available this run; not opted in.

#1 · TOP PICK

Agent Broadcast Arena

Axis: Agents & content Confidence: 86% Complexity: Medium–High

Make Battle a stadium, not a lying matchmaker. Primary session: agent-vs-agent (or agent-assisted) bouts with spectator list, LLM commentary, shareable replay URLs, and one-tap battle highlight video into the generator gallery. Humans pick agents / approve rematches; they do not wait in a fake human queue. Microtransactions here are narrative rights — clean export, trophy frames, commentary skins, season pass cosmetics — never house-AI outcome multipliers.

Basis: direct: spectator + replay share under /vidgenerator/battle/replay/... + battle_hunt_video + battle_agents_match exist but are unwired from the hot path. external: Clawosseum-style agent arenas; MIDiA spectator monetization. Arena plan forbids RM on house AI.

Rationale: Aligns Battle with agents + video (the real platform gravity) and answers microtx without pay-to-win RNG.

Downsides: Needs product focus and wiring work; empty spectate feed until agents are scheduled; “narrative rights” SKUs must be invented in shop config.

#2

Ghost Ladder (zero concurrent humans)

Axis: Core loop Confidence: 82% Complexity: Medium

Delete the fake queue. Every fight is against an async ghost (recorded stance / prior match). Label free AI warm-ups as Instant Skirmish. Streaks, trophies, and leaderboards stay; live matchmaking does not pretend to exist.

Basis: direct: UI polls for queue fields the API never returns; PvP branch still uses weighted random. external: async ghost / time-trial patterns.

Rationale: Restores trust — the cheapest “something new and fresh” that is still Battle-as-play.

Downsides: Feels less “esports” until ghosts populate; still needs clear UX copy.

#3

Delete the destination, keep the verbs

Axis: Surfaces Confidence: 78% Complexity: Medium

Retire /battle as a nav destination. Keep quick-battle, progress, trophies, and pass progression as embeds inside Generator / Hunter Game / frontpage Game Hub (Heardle-inside-flagship pattern). Competition survives; the stub SPA does not.

Basis: direct: split Game+Battle favorites, bridge widgets already deep-link; PROJECT_RETHINK consolidate. external: Discord Activities / Spotify Heardle embed; HubSpot-style feature kill.

Rationale: Clean answer to “delete” without throwing away competitive identity.

Downsides: Nav/comms work; risk of orphaning battlegrounds/champions-league leftovers.

#4

Compliant microtx rails (not P2W skirmish)

Axis: Monetization rails Confidence: 84% Complexity: Low–Medium

If Battle stays monetized: (1) wire skirmish wins into existing battle-pass XP, (2) kill on-page MN2 faucet, (3) RM only on human-pot escrow/tournament rake, (4) sell film/cosmetics flat via shop. Do not require full Arena HTML merge before taking money on those rails.

Basis: direct: Arena compliance § house AI; shop pass quests omit battle today; MN2 claim UI on battle page; buy-in routes already on battle blueprint.

Rationale: Answers “use for microtransactions” without repeating GameFi anti-patterns.

Downsides: Revenue may stay modest until content/ghost loops produce repeat sessions.

#5

Bounty Desk (not battlefield)

Axis: Core loop Confidence: 74% Complexity: Medium

Redefine Battle as contest-against-world: timed bounties on generator jobs, hunt quotas, agent skill checks — scored challenges, not empty lobbies. Optional weekly same-prompt contests with leaderboards.

Basis: external: Axie bounty participation outdrawing pure PvP. direct: matchmaker fiction in code; Hunter/vidgenerator already move.

Rationale: Fresh purpose that feeds the flagship loop instead of competing with it.

Downsides: Naming/branding conflict with “Battle”; overlaps quest systems.

#6

Seed-bank hibernation

Axis: Surfaces Confidence: 80% Complexity: Low

If reinvent is not next: freeze Battle UI behind a hibernation banner, demote nav, keep API seeds (progress, arena economy, pass, replay/video). Schedule a kill review. Stub tabs gone from the surface now.

Basis: external: LoR PvP hibernation. direct: stub route headers + salvageable services.

Rationale: Lowest-risk way to stop lying to users without burning future options.

Downsides: Looks like retreat; requires clear public copy.

#7

One Progress Passport

Axis: Progress identity Confidence: 77% Complexity: Low–Medium

Whatever destiny wins, make /api/battle/progress the only Battle “save game,” status-match into Hunter XP, and stop rendering a guilt-trip of missing_for_v2 on the hero. Agents and UI share one contract.

Basis: direct: battle-v2-requirements one progress contract; dual write to points + award_xp already exists.

Rationale: Enabler that compounds any of #1–#6; not a full destiny alone.

Downsides: Incremental; easy to under-scope as “just API polish.”
